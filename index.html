Neon Swarm

Fly the drone. Survive the swarm!

A free survival shooter for your browser

Your drone is alone in a neon arena and the enemies keep coming. It fires on its own, so you just steer. Grab the gems they drop to level up and pick one of three upgrades: multishot, piercing bullets, orbiting orbs, shockwaves and more. Survive a swarm every 45 seconds and a boss every two minutes, then spend your coins on permanent upgrades so each run gets you further.

How to play

  • Phone or tablet: drag anywhere to move (or switch to a joystick).
  • Computer: drag with the mouse or use the arrow keys. Esc to pause.

Free to play. No download, no sign-up.
